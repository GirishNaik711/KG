## Example 1: Presentation — AI Transformation Services {#ex1}

### Slide 1 — Title Slide
**Headline:** "Complex transformations demand *collaboration*"

**Visual:**
- Dark background (#282728)
- Circular motif with flowing lines in green-to-blue gradient
- Cropped at edges for dynamic feel

**Typography:**
- "Complex transformations demand" — Open Sans Regular, white
- "*collaboration*" — Stix Two Text Semibold Italic, #86EB22

**Logo:** Primary lockup, stamp placement, lower right

### Slide 2 — Problem Statement
**Headline:** "AI initiatives that *advance* business"

**Content:**
- 3-4 bullet points on challenges (Open Sans Regular)
- Photography: Business environment showing technology integration
- Circle-cut photo treatment

**Visual:**
- Light background with subtle grain
- Photography in circular frame
- Supporting circular motifs in corners

### Slide 3 — Deloitte Approach
**Headline:** "Bringing *together* strategy, technology, and change"

**Visual:**
- Three overlapping circles showing disciplines (avoid two-circle Mastercard pattern)
- Each circle different shade of green
- Center overlap highlighted

### Slide 4 — Outcomes/Impact
**Headline:** "Measurable *impact* across industries"

**Visual:**
- Photography showing business outcomes (full-bleed or circle-cut)
- Data points overlaid

### Slide 5 — Call to Action
**Headline:** "Ready to *advance* your AI transformation?"

**Visual:**
- Clean, green background
- Simple circular motif
- Professional photo (collaboration theme)

## Example 2: LinkedIn Post — Industry Insights {#ex2}

### Option A: Professional Insight

"The most successful digital transformations don't start with technology—they start with bringing together the right perspectives.

When business strategy meets technical expertise, when organizational change aligns with implementation, progress moves from possible to inevitable.

Our latest research shows companies taking this integrated approach see 3x faster time-to-value. Because difficult alone is achievable *together*.

[Link] #DigitalTransformation #BusinessStrategy #DeloitteInsights"

**Why it works:** Leads with insight, shows what's coming together (strategy + tech + org change), demonstrates progress (3x faster), conversational but professional.

### Option B: Thought Leadership

"Just attended an incredible summit on sustainable manufacturing. The conversation that stuck with me:

'One company can innovate. An ecosystem can transform an entire industry.'

When competitors become collaborators on what matters most, everyone advances.

#Sustainability #Manufacturing #IndustryTransformation"

**Why it works:** Human opening, real insight, collaboration at scale, platform themes natural (convene, alliances, advance), genuine optimism.

Our approach brings together audit domain expertise, machine learning, and continuous quality controls. The result isn't just faster analysis—it's deeper insight that advances audit quality.

Early adopters are seeing:
- 40% reduction in sampling time

This service is designed with independence and quality standards at the core. All analytics are auditor-controlled, fully explainable, and integrate seamlessly with existing audit workflows.

[Name]

## Tone Calibration: Before & After {#tone}

### Too Corporate → On-Brand
**Before:** "Deloitte leverages its multidisciplinary capabilities to deliver synergistic outcomes for enterprise-level digital transformation engagements."
**After:** "Complex transformations demand more than technology. They require bringing together business strategy, technical expertise, and organizational change. When these disciplines work in concert, AI implementations deliver measurable results."
**What changed:** Dropped jargon ("synergistic," "leverages"), added human specificity, showed what's coming together and what progress looks like.

### Too Casual → On-Brand
**Before:** "We're really excited about this cool new AI tool! It's going to be amazing for everyone!"
**After:** "Our new AI analytics platform brings together audit expertise and machine learning to advance how teams uncover insights—faster, and with the rigor our work demands."
**What changed:** Replaced vague excitement with specific insight, showed the "together" element (expertise + ML), connected to impact.

### Too Salesy → On-Brand
**Before:** "Deloitte is the #1 choice for AI transformation. Choose us for unmatched results!"
**After:** "AI transformations succeed when strategy, technology, and change management work in concert. That's the approach our clients trust—and the approach that delivers measurable outcomes."
**What changed:** Removed self-congratulatory claims, led with insight, showed differentiation through approach rather than assertion.

### Missing Platform Connection → On-Brand
**Before:** "Our tax advisory team helps you with compliance and planning."
**After:** "Tax challenges don't exist in isolation. They intersect with business strategy, technology infrastructure, and regulatory change. Deloitte Tax's multidisciplinary model means specialists collaborate across these domains—so tax solutions advance your broader business objectives."
**What changed:** Connected to "together" (disciplines intersecting), showed "progress" (advancing business objectives), demonstrated MDM value.

## Common Scenarios & Edge Cases {#scenarios}

### Regulated Business (A&A Client Deliverable)
**DO:** Standard Deloitte logo (no lockup), follow all other brand guidelines, reference A&A guardrails
**DON'T:** Use "Together makes progress" lockup — independence concerns

### Sub-Brand (Deloitte Digital Campaign)
**DO:** Deloitte Digital wordmark, follow visual system (colors, motifs, typography), include Deloitte green
**DON'T:** Add tagline lockup alongside sub-brand

### Co-Branded Alliance (Deloitte + AWS)
**DO:** Standard Deloitte logo, balance both brands equitably, partnership tone
**DON'T:** Use tagline lockup next to partner logo

### Quick Internal Email
**DO:** Concise, human tone, standard logo in signature
**DON'T:** Over-design, add visual elements, use tagline lockup in signature

### Major Client Presentation
**DO:** Full brand treatment, all five codes on cover, consistent visual system, platform messaging
**DON'T:** Overwhelm with branding, use tagline excessively, sacrifice clarity for flourish

## Quick Reference Content Checklist {#checklist}

### Before Creating
- [ ] Who is the audience?
- [ ] What is the purpose?
- [ ] Primary surface or supporting content?
- [ ] Any restrictions (A&A, Tax, co-branding)?
- [ ] What is coming together?
- [ ] What progress are we making?

### After Creating
- [ ] Message aligns with *Together makes progress* spirit
- [ ] Tone is clear, confident, and human
- [ ] Visual elements include Deloitte Green
- [ ] Typography follows brand rules
- [ ] Logo lockup used appropriately
- [ ] No restricted usage violations
- [ ] Quality meets Deloitte standards
