# Messaging Details

## Complete Brand Narrative

The "Together makes progress" platform addresses the complexity and challenges facing businesses today:

**Global imperative:**
- Progress of any kind is hard to make
- Today's challenges are deeply complex
- Opportunities are often fleeting
- But *together* is the force that can change everything

**MDM approach:**
- Together, we see problems from multiple angles
- MDM reveals what no one can see alone
- Works as a magnet and force multiplier

**Character + Results:**
- Clients trust we'll bring necessary perspectives, skills, and technologies
- With speed, scale and integrity
- Confidence in what we do and stand for

**Impact:**
- Hard-won progress isn't just made by us
- It matters to all

## Voice and Personality Principles

### Clarity Comes First
- All communication should be clear, concise and have a point of view
- Avoid jargon unless audience-appropriate
- Get to the point quickly

### Insightful Beats Clever
- Generating "catch phrases" to define services as separate "brands" is less effective
- Apply core Deloitte brand to convey unique insights, approaches and services
- Focus on substance over style

### Be Human
- Always aim for conversational, person-to-person style
- Fits the tone of the tagline
- Stays true to brand voice
- Avoid overly formal or corporate language

### Show Genuine Optimism
- Deloitte brand is positive with high emotional quotient
- Seek to move things forward
- No matter how difficult the moment, express genuine optimism
- Face change with confidence

### Meet People Where They Are
- Context and audience matter
- Understand audience needs and knowledge level
- Communicate accordingly
- Adjust technical depth appropriately

### Convey Purpose
- Purpose is foundational to positioning
- Goal to make impact should not be lost
- Show impact on project, client, industry, or business world

## Protecting the Tagline

### Grammar Is Intentional
The tagline uses a provocative grammatical structure:
- "Together makes progress" NOT "Together, we make progress"
- Like Apple's "Think Different" or AT&T's "Rethink possible"
- Signifies confidence in point of view
- Makes it more ownable and memorable

### Full Tagline Only
**Do NOT create variations:**
- ✗ Together makes quality
- ✗ Connection makes progress  
- ✗ Together sparks progress
- ✗ TMP (acronym)

### Prevent Overuse
Speak to core ideas behind the tagline rather than using "together," "makes," or "progress" repeatedly in headlines, copy or scripts.

Use alternative vocabulary (see main SKILL.md).

## Storytelling Examples

### Example 1: Service Offering
**Bad:** "Deloitte's AI Advisory helps companies implement AI solutions."

**Good:** "Complex AI transformations demand more than technology. They require bringing together business strategy, technical expertise, and organizational change management. When these disciplines work in concert, AI implementations don't just deploy—they deliver measurable business outcomes that advance entire industries."

**Why it works:**
- Addresses "what's coming together" (business + tech + org change)
- Shows "what progress" (measurable outcomes, industry advancement)
- Human, conversational tone
- Conveys purpose and impact

### Example 2: Talent/Internal
**Bad:** "Join Deloitte and work with smart people."

**Good:** "The biggest impacts aren't made alone. At Deloitte, your expertise meets diverse perspectives, collaborative problem-solving, and multidisciplinary teams. Together, we turn complex challenges into breakthrough solutions."

**Why it works:**
- Speaks to platform themes (multidisciplinary, collaborative)
- Genuine optimism
- Clear benefit/purpose
- Human voice

### Example 3: Industry Insight
**Bad:** "Healthcare is changing with new technology."

**Good:** "Healthcare transformation happens when clinical expertise meets data science, when patient outcomes inform technology design, when providers and payers align on value. This convergence isn't simple—but it's how we advance toward a healthcare system that works for everyone."

**Why it works:**
- Multiple elements coming together (clinical + data + providers)
- Clear progress outcome (better healthcare system)
- Purposeful and impactful
- Insightful, not clever

## Content-Specific Messaging

### Presentations
- Clear narrative arc
- Each slide answers part of storytelling framework
- Titles use platform vocabulary
- Balance data with human impact
- End with clear call to action or takeaway

### Social Media
- Lead with the insight or outcome
- Keep conversational
- Can be shorter but still substantive
- Show genuine optimism
- When using visuals, ensure they support the message

### Emails
- Clear subject line with purpose
- Person-to-person opening
- Context-appropriate formality
- Genuine, optimistic tone
- Clear call-to-action
- Adapt to recipient's role and knowledge

### Marketing Materials
- Headline emphasizes "together" or "progress"
- Support with concrete examples or outcomes
- Show what's coming together
- Demonstrate the impact
- Balance aspiration with authenticity

## Writing for Different Audiences

### C-Suite/Leadership
- Focus on business outcomes and transformational impact
- Strategic perspective
- Industry-level implications
- Measurable results and scale
- Purpose-driven language

### Practitioners/Technical
- Balance technical depth with accessibility
- Show how collaboration enhances technical work
- Specific examples of MDM in action
- Innovation and problem-solving focus
- Respect their expertise

### Clients
- Address their specific challenges
- Show how Deloitte's approach is differentiated
- Demonstrate understanding of their industry/context
- Balance confidence with partnership
- Clear value proposition

### Internal/Employees
- Authentic and energizing
- Shows pride in the work
- Emphasizes impact and purpose
- Recognizes challenges while staying optimistic
- Builds sense of collective mission

## Deloitte Terminology Glossary

Use these terms correctly in brand communications:

| Term | Meaning | Usage Notes |
|------|---------|-------------|
| **MDM** | Multidisciplinary Model — Deloitte's approach of bringing multiple disciplines together | Core to brand platform; emphasize as differentiator |
| **Alliances & Ecosystems** | Partner relationships (AWS, ServiceNow, SAP, etc.) | Co-branding rules apply; no tagline lockup |
| **Advise, Implement, Operate** | Three modes of Deloitte engagement | Use when describing end-to-end capability |
| **Restricted Entities** | Audit clients with independence restrictions | Never use tagline lockup on their materials |
| **Sub-brands** | Deloitte Digital, Deloitte Legal, etc. | Have own visual identities; no tagline lockup |
| **Enterprise Solutions (ES)** | Internal shared services functions | Internal audience; lighter brand treatment |
| **Operate** | Managed services / ongoing client operations | Emphasize sustained impact and transformation |
| **Brand Codes** | Five required elements on primary surfaces: Logo, Color, Personality, Circle, Tagline | All five must appear on covers and landing pages |
| **Primary Surface** | Covers, landing pages, event installations — first-impression touchpoints | Full brand treatment required |

### Industry-Specific Messaging Notes

When writing for specific industries, adapt the "together" and "progress" themes:

- **Financial Services:** Regulatory + technology + risk management coming together → stability, compliance, innovation
- **Technology/TMT:** Speed + scale + human insight → market transformation, product innovation
- **Government/Public Sector:** Policy + technology + citizen needs → public impact, modernization
- **Life Sciences/Healthcare:** Clinical + data + patient outcomes → better health outcomes, access
- **Energy/Resources:** Sustainability + technology + operations → transition, resilience, efficiency
